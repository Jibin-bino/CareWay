Diabetes

Endocrinology & Metabolism

What is it?

A chronic condition where the body cannot properly process blood sugar (glucose) due to insufficient or ineffective insulin production.

Key Symptoms

  • Frequent urination & thirst
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Blurry vision & fatigue
  • Slow-healing wounds

Types

  • Type 1 — Autoimmune, insulin needed
  • Type 2 — Lifestyle-linked, most common
  • Gestational — During pregnancy

Management Tips

  • Monitor blood sugar regularly
  • Low-glycemic, balanced diet
  • 30 min exercise daily
  • Take medications as prescribed

Thyroid Health

Endocrinology & Hormonal Health

What is it?

The thyroid is a butterfly-shaped gland in the neck that produces hormones regulating metabolism, heart rate, and energy levels.

Common Conditions

  • Hypothyroidism — Underactive; fatigue, weight gain
  • Hyperthyroidism — Overactive; rapid heart rate
  • Hashimoto's — Autoimmune attack on thyroid
  • Goiter — Enlarged thyroid gland

Key Symptoms

  • Unexplained weight changes
  • Extreme fatigue or hyperactivity
  • Hair loss or brittle nails
  • Neck swelling or hoarse voice

Management Tips

  • Get a TSH blood test if symptomatic
  • Take levothyroxine as prescribed
  • Eat iodine-rich foods (dairy, fish)
  • Regular endocrinologist follow-up

Heart Health

Cardiology & Cardiovascular Wellness

What is it?

Cardiovascular disease affects the heart and blood vessels. Up to 80% of premature heart disease is preventable through healthy lifestyle choices.

Warning Signs

  • Chest pain or tightness
  • Shortness of breath
  • Irregular heartbeat
  • Swelling in legs or ankles

Risk Factors

  • High blood pressure & cholesterol
  • Smoking & excessive alcohol
  • Obesity & inactivity
  • Diabetes & chronic stress

Prevention Tips

  • Eat heart-healthy (low sodium, low fat)
  • Exercise 150 min/week minimum
  • Quit smoking completely
  • Check BP & cholesterol regularly

Blood Pressure

Hypertension & Cardiovascular Risk

What is it?

Blood pressure measures the force of blood against artery walls. Normal is ~120/80 mmHg. Consistently above 140/90 mmHg indicates hypertension.

Risk Factors

  • High salt & poor diet
  • Physical inactivity & obesity
  • Smoking & excess alcohol
  • Family history & ageing

Symptoms

  • Often called the "silent killer" — no symptoms
  • Severe cases: headache, blurred vision
  • Nosebleeds in very high readings
  • Dizziness or chest pain

Management Tips

  • Reduce salt to <2,300 mg/day
  • Exercise 30 min daily
  • Take prescribed medication
  • Monitor BP at home regularly

Nutrition

Diet, Metabolism & Healthy Eating

Why it matters

A balanced diet provides the energy and nutrients your body needs. No single food has everything — variety, portion control and consistency are key.

Core Nutrients

  • Carbs — Energy; prefer whole grains
  • Protein — Muscle & immune; lean meats, legumes
  • Healthy Fats — Brain; nuts, olive oil, fish
  • Fibre — Digestion; veg, fruits, pulses

Signs of Poor Nutrition

  • Constant fatigue or brain fog
  • Frequent illness or slow healing
  • Hair loss or brittle nails
  • Unexplained weight gain or loss

Practical Tips

  • Half your plate = vegetables & fruit
  • Choose whole grains over refined
  • Drink 8 glasses of water daily
  • Limit processed food & added sugar

Wellness

Mental Health, Sleep & Lifestyle Balance

What is Wellness?

Wellness is an active process of building healthy habits across physical, mental and emotional dimensions — not just the absence of illness.

Pillars of Wellness

  • Sleep — 7–9 hours per night
  • Movement — Daily exercise reduces anxiety
  • Mindfulness — Reduces chronic stress
  • Connection — Social bonds support longevity

Mental Health Signs

  • Persistent sadness or low mood
  • Loss of interest in activities
  • Sleep disturbances or fatigue
  • Difficulty concentrating

Daily Wellness Habits

  • Keep a consistent sleep schedule
  • Take a 20 min walk every day
  • Limit screen time before bed
  • Seek help early — don't wait
